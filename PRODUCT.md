# ASEAN Demand Radar

Tells a small business which product to test in which ASEAN country, what to change, and whether the test worked. Source of truth: https://claude.ai/artifact/LHUiMetzrGq5sEVRYZanUh

- **Users:** an ebook seller or small business marketer. Judges see it on a projector during a hackathon demo.
- **Mode:** Operate. The user completes a task, moving from a book page to a reviewed test kit.
- **Register:** direct and honest. Scores are "demand signals" and outputs are "recommended tests", never predictions.
- **Must always show:** sources behind every score, `no evidence yet` for gaps, labels on sample and replayed data, and human approval before content is used.
- **Avoid:** stereotypes about countries, invented numbers, generic AI dashboard styling.
